📖 Financial Words Made Super Simple

Here is a quick cheat-sheet for financial terms. Bookmark this anytime you are researching stocks!

🏷️ Stock Ticker
A short 1-to-4 letter abbreviation used by stock exchanges. E.g., AAPL for Apple, KO for Coca-Cola, MSFT for Microsoft.
🏬 Market Cap (Company Size)
Short for Market Capitalization. Total market price tag of all shares. A $2 Trillion company is massive and usually very established.
💵 Dividend
Cash paid directly to shareholders out of company profits, usually every 3 months. Like getting cash back for owning part of the business!
📊 P/E Ratio (Price to Earnings)
How many dollars you pay for $1 of company annual profit. Lower P/E means bargain price; higher P/E means investors expect fast growth.
🎢 Volatility
How wildly stock prices bounce up and down day to day. High volatility = bigger price swings; Low volatility = calm and stable.
🛡️ Defensive Stock
A business that stays stable even during recessions because people always buy their products (e.g. food, electricity, healthcare).
🌱 Long-Term Growth Horizon
An investment timeframe of 10 to 30+ years. Short-term price dips matter less because compounding business growth has decades to flourish.
⚖️ Balanced Horizon
A mid-term investment timeframe balancing growth with risk management, focusing on stable market leaders with manageable downside.
🛡️ Capital Preservation & Income
A focus on protecting saved principal, avoiding high stock price swings, and generating steady quarterly dividend cash flow.
📈 RSI (Relative Strength Index)
A momentum indicator scored from 0 to 100. Below 30 suggests a stock is oversold (cheap/bargain), above 70 suggests overbought, and 30–70 reflects healthy momentum.
📉 SMA (Simple Moving Average)
The average stock price over 50 or 200 days. When the current price is above its 50-day or 200-day SMA, it signals a strong upward market trend.
⚡ Beta (Market Volatility)
Measures how much a stock moves compared to the S&P 500 index. Beta of 1.0 moves equally with the market; >1.0 means bigger price swings; <1.0 means calm defensive stability.
📊 MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)
A trend-following momentum indicator showing the relationship between two moving averages of a stock's price, signaling buyer or seller momentum.
🎯 Wall St. Target & Analyst Upside
The 12-month average price target set by institutional equity research analysts (Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, J.P. Morgan).
📈 Short Float & Squeeze Risk
The percentage of total shares shorted by hedge funds. Short float above 15% indicates potential for a rapid short squeeze rally.
🏛️ Institutional Ownership
The percentage of stock owned by smart-money institutions, hedge funds, mutual funds, and 13F filings.
🌱 ESG Rating (Environmental, Social & Governance)
Measures how responsibly a company treats the planet (E), employees & communities (S), and corporate ethics & board oversight (G). Green = clean & ethical; Yellow = average; Red = high environmental or governance risk.
